> Open‑source archive for the quadruped robot \(Team 1\) project of Suzhou University of Technology, ROBOCON Season 2025
> 
> 

## Introduction

This repository archives the software, control schemes and supporting documents for **Suzhou University of Technology ROBOCON 2025 Quadruped Robot Team 1**\. The 2025 season marked our university’s very first appearance at the ROBOCON competition, which carries great commemorative significance\.

> Note: I am a member of the electrical control group for the 2026 season and did not participate in the development work of the 2025 season\. No functional modifications have been made to the original code in this repository\. This work only serves for archiving, sorting and organization, aiming to provide reference ideas for other participating teams\.
> 
> 

Competition results of the 2025 season: **National Third‑Class Award for all three events**\.

Core technical directions:

- Quadruped robot motion control

- Robot body position control

## Important Notice

Since the organizer of this repository is not an original developer from the 2025 season, some original design documents, debugging logs and internal notes are missing\.
