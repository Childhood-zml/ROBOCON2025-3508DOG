> ROBOCON 2025 Legged Robot \(Team 1\) Project Open‑source Repository, Suzhou University of Technology
> 
> 

## Introduction

This repository archives and organizes the software, control schemes and supporting materials for **Suzhou University of Technology ROBOCON 2025 Legged Robot Team 1**\. The 2025 season marked our university’s very first participation in the ROBOCON competition, making it a milestone event\.

> Note: As a member of the 2026 Electrical Control Team, I was not involved in the original 2025‑season development\. No functional modifications have been made to the original source code in this repository\. This work is purely for archiving, sorting and documentation, intended to provide reference ideas for other competing teams\.
> 
> 

2025 Competition Result: **National Third Prize for all three events**

Core Technical Directions:

- Legged robot motion control

- Robot body position control
